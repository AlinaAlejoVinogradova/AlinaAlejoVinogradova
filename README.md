## 🌿 About Me

I'm a plant scientist and bioinformatician with an MSc in Plant Science from **King Abdullah University of Science and Technology (KAUST), Saudi Arabia**, and a BSc in Agricultural Engineering, specializing in Plant Breeding, from **Universidad Autónoma Chapingo, Mexico**.

My experience spans transcriptomics, RNA-seq pipeline development, gene regulatory network analysis, and statistical approaches to genomic prediction. I work across the bioinformatics workflow, from raw sequencing data processing to downstream statistical analysis and biological interpretation, including computational workflows on high-performance computing (HPC) systems.

## 🔬 Research Experience

### MSc Thesis — Comparative Transcriptomics of Saltgrass

**KAUST · Plant Breeding and Genetics Lab · 2024–2026**

Transcriptomic analyses of *Distichlis spicata* and *Distichlis stricta* under high salinity.

- Developed and executed end-to-end RNA-seq analysis pipelines on HPC systems.
- Investigated comparative transcriptomic responses of two non-domesticated halophyte species.
- Contributed to experimental research planning and the coordination of plant germplasm maintenance and data collection.

### BSc Thesis — Gene Regulatory Networks in Tomato

**Universidad Autónoma Chapingo · 2019–2023**

Identification of gene regulatory networks related to drought tolerance in tomato (*Solanum lycopersicum* L.).

- Developed automated transcriptomic and network analysis pipelines on HPC clusters.
- Applied DIANE for gene regulatory network inference.
- Combined computational analysis with biological interpretation to investigate candidate regulatory mechanisms associated with drought tolerance.

### Research & Bioinformatics Experience

**CIMMYT · Genomic Prediction Internship**

- Applied Bayesian statistical models for genomic prediction in wheat (*Triticum aestivum*) and maize (*Zea mays*).

**Ecology Institute, UNAM · Bioinformatics Internship**

- Analyzed RNA-seq data in R in an epigenetics research group studying *Arabidopsis thaliana*.
- Co-developed and co-instructed a workshop on R statistical packages and tidyverse for researchers.
